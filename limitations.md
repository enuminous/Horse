# Limitations and Falsification

HORSE is an experimental human–system evaluation framework.

## Limitations

- Human state is difficult to measure reliably.
- Trust is context-dependent.
- Workload proxies can be crude.
- Counterfactual override quality is often unavailable.
- Human and system errors may be confounded.
- Strong performance can mask poor calibration.

## Falsification criteria

HORSE's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Human-only and system-only metrics predict outcomes equally well.
2. Trust calibration adds no value.
3. Handoff metrics do not correlate with operational outcomes.
4. Recursive coupling history adds no value.
5. Results fail across different task domains.
6. High HORSE scores do not correspond to better team performance.

## Claim boundary

HORSE evaluates observable human–system operational structure. It does not infer private mental states beyond the supplied measurements.
